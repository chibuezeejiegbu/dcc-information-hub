# DCC Information Hub --- Forms \& Registration

## Scope

Public DCC forms visible on the official Forms index, with purpose,
known deadlines/requirements, fields, and direct links.

## Bot rule

Never invent a deadline, eligibility rule, approval process, or missing
form URL. If the form page does not state a deadline or requirement, say
**"No deadline/requirement is stated on the accessible form page."** If
a user needs confirmation or a form is unavailable, offer to connect
them with a human DCC team member.

## Forms index

Official index: https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms

### 1. Interest Groups

* **Purpose:** Join a DCC interest group.
* **Categories:** Entertainment \& Lifestyle; Politics and Government;
Sport and Fitness; Spirituality.
* **Centre:** Mainland, Island, or Both.
* **Fields:** First name, last name, phone, email, interest group,
centre.
* **Deadline:** Not stated.
* **Link:** https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/interest-groups

### 2. First Timers

* **Purpose:** Register/identify as a first-time visitor and provide
follow-up information.
* **Fields:** Worship location; service date; first/last name;
address; email; mobile; marital status; gender; occupation; age
range; office address; who invited you; inviter mobile number;
first-time status; desire for DCC membership; prayer request;
suggestions/comments.
* **Deadline:** Not stated.
* **Link:** https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/first-timer

### 3. Prayer Request

* **Purpose:** Submit a prayer request.
* **Fields:** Worship location; first/last name; email; mobile; member
status; whether follow-up is required; prayer details.
* **Deadline:** Not stated.
* **Link:** https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/prayer-request

### 4. Testimony

* **Purpose:** Submit a testimony.
* **Fields:** Worship location; name; mobile; email; gender; date;
"What did the Lord do?"
* **Deadline:** Not stated.
* **Link:** https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/testimony-form

### 5. Child Naming

* **Purpose:** Register for child naming.
* **Deadline:** Must be filled **72 hours before the naming**.
* **Guardian requirements/fields:** Household name; father's
name/mobile; mother's name/mobile; email; whether both are married;
whether they are DCC members; marriage date; home address.
* The form then proceeds to service and child details.
* **Link:** https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/child-naming

### 6. Child Dedication

* **Purpose:** Register for child dedication.
* **Deadline:** Complete by the Wednesday before the last Sunday of the month and at least one week before dedication Sunday.
Late submissions are automatically moved to the following month.
* **Guardian fields:** Household name; father/mother names and
mobiles; email; marriage status; DCC membership; marriage date; home
address.
* **Link:**
https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/child-dedication

### 7. Wedding Anniversary

* **Purpose:** Register a wedding anniversary/thanksgiving request.
* **Fields:** Couple's name; contact address; husband/wife mobile
numbers; DCC member status; years married; worker status; whether
they married in DCC; wedding date; proposed thanksgiving date;
venue; pictures.
* **Deadline:** Not stated.
* **Link:**
https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/wedding-anniversary

### 8. Newly Wed / Wedding Thanksgiving

* **Purpose:** Celebrate the success of a wedding ceremony in church
through a thanksgiving service.
* **Date rule:** Proposed thanksgiving date can only be the **first
Sunday of the month** for both Island and Mainland centres; other
dates will not be acknowledged.
* **Deadline:** Form must be filled **one week before the due date**.
* **Follow-up:** Contact Admin on **08028356363 after submission**.
* **Fields:** Couple's name; contact address; husband/wife mobiles;
email; DCC member status; worker status; whether married in DCC;
wedding date; proposed thanksgiving date; venue; pictures.
* **Link:**
https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/wedding-thanksgiving

### 9. Water Baptism

* **Purpose:** Register for water baptism.
* **Fields:** Name; email; mobile; residential address; gender; date
of new birth; Discovery School completion; worship centre.
* **Deadline:** Not stated on the accessible page.
* **Link:** https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/water-baptism

### 10. Letter of Introduction / Reference

* **Purpose:** Request a DCC letter of introduction/reference.
* **Fields:** Full name; mobile; marital status; email; address;
purpose; DCC membership; completion status for Discovery School, The
Journey, Membership Class, and Prayer School; picture upload;
certification.
* **Deadline:** Not stated.
* **Link:**
https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/letter-of-introduction

### 11. Facility Usage

* **Purpose:** Request use of a DCC facility.
* **Deadline:** Request must be made **at least 48 hours in advance**.
* **Fields:** Name; email; mobile; department/fellowship; purpose;
facility location; expected capacity; availability; start/end dates
and times; event frequency; setup/agreement steps.
* **Link:** https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/forms/facility-usage

### 12. Department Leadership Appointment and Replacement

* **Purpose:** Request appointment/replacement of a department leader.
* **Requester roles:** Workforce Leadership, Shepherd, HOD, Assistant HOD,
Other.
* **Centre:** Island or Mainland.
* **Department:** Select from DCC's department structure.
* **Proposed leader roles:** Shepherd, HOD, Assistant HOD, Unit Leader,
Other.
* **Deadline:** Not stated.
* **Link:**
https://forms.davidschristiancentre.org/department-leadership-appointment-and-replacement-form/

## Public-form gaps

* The accessible Forms index lists the public forms above.
* No separate public "registration deadline" is stated for most forms.
* "Membership Class" appears as a completion status in the Letter of
Introduction form, but no separate public Membership Class form is
listed on the Forms index reviewed.
* "Discovery Tent"/First Steps is described elsewhere as ongoing at
services, but it is not treated here as an additional public form
unless a direct form is exposed.
