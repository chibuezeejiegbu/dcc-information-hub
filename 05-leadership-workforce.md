## Workforce hierarchy

The Department Leadership Appointment and Replacement Form uses this
hierarchy, highest to lowest: 1. **Workforce Leadership** --- oversees
appointments and replacements across departments. 2. **Shepherd** ---
senior departmental oversight. 3. **HOD (Head of Department)** ---
day-to-day departmental leadership. 4. **Asst. HOD** --- supports the
HOD. 5. **Unit Leader** --- leads a specific unit within a department.
