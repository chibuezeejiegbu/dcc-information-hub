# DCC Information Hub --- Ministries, Fellowships & Interest Groups

## Scope

Alternative church expressions, ministries, fellowships, online
community, and the public Interest Groups structure.

## Bot rule

Use only stated target audiences, programmes, times, and leadership.
Where a meeting time or leader is not documented, say so. Do not infer
leadership from social-media ownership. If the user needs current
schedules, membership rules, or a contact not provided here, offer human
escalation.

## Alternative church expressions

### Preserved Generation

-   Children's ministry expression for children **birth to 12 years**.
-   Mission: safe environment; applicable, true, fun teaching of the
    Word of Faith.
-   Vision: partner with parents to raise children who love God, stand
    for His Word, have a real relationship with Him, and are victorious.
-   **Pastor in charge:** Pastor Shade Fayomi.
-   **Child registration:** https://bit.ly/PREGENregistration
-   **Instagram:** @dcckidsmin
-   **YouTube:** PeeGee TV
-   **Operational gap:** The accessible page currently repeats general
    ministry description in several FAQ areas, so exact check-in, class
    progression, pickup, and service-operation answers should be
    confirmed directly with Preserved Generation.

-   Focus: connection, shared experiences, growth, learning,
    empowerment.
-   Started as a column in a relationship newsletter and evolved into a
    global female empowerment platform.
-   Official site: https://www.justusgirlsglobal.org/
