## DCC Schools

Official site: https://schools.davidschristiancentre.org/

DCC Schools identifies itself as the **discipleship arm of David's
Christian Centre** and says it spiritually equips people with
scripture-based tools for ministry and leadership.

### Discovery School

-   Foundational discipleship level.
-   Values: Real Life, Right Living, Results, Reaching Out.
-   Prepares believers for service.

### Prayer School

-   Teaches believers to pray consistently and practically.

### Journey School

-   Ministry school covering marriage, spiritual growth, ministry of
    Christ, the Holy Spirit, and Kingdom principles.

### Leadership Academy

-   Develops leaders at every level in DCC.
-   Emphasises culture, influence, and service.
