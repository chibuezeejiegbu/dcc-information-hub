# DCC Information Hub --- External Ecosystem & Socials

## Scope

Official DCC-linked subsites, partner/ministry sites, external resource
channels, and public social handles documented in the knowledge base.

## Bot rule

Treat external sites as separate sources. Their information may have its
own update cycle and may conflict with DCC's main site. Do not merge
their contact details into DCC's main-site details without attribution.
If a link or handle cannot be verified, state that clearly and offer
human escalation.

## Source hierarchy for the bot

When sources conflict: 1. Prefer the current main DCC website for
DCC-wide operational facts. 2. Prefer a specialised official DCC page
for that specific ministry/form/programme. 3. Use DCC-linked subsites
for their own programme-specific details. 4. Use social profiles for
public-handle verification and promotional context, not as the sole
source for operational policies. 5. Treat third-party/press coverage as
attributed external reporting. 6. If the conflict remains unresolved,
say so and offer human escalation.
