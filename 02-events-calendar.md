# DCC Information Hub --- Events & Calendar

## Scope

Retrieval-ready calendar covering recurring DCC programmes, historical August–September 2026 anniversary events, October 2026 sermon topics, and the confirmed Career Prep 2026 details.

## Bot rule

Treat past dates as **historical**, not upcoming. The September 2026
calendar belongs to DCC's 30th-anniversary season. October 2026 sermon topics and Career Prep 2026 details are listed below. Do not invent
current/future dates when the live event feed is unavailable. If a user
asks about an event whose current date, registration status, venue, or
logistics are not confirmed here, offer to connect them with a human DCC
team member.

## Website event-feed status

The live DCC Events page is accessible, but its text currently exposes
only "Upcoming event" / "Loading Upcoming events..". It therefore cannot
be treated as a complete scraped current-event list.

## Recurring programmes

### Gaining Momentum

-   Annual kickoff event, held every January.
-   DCC describes it as a spiritually charged gathering involving
    prayer, worship, and teaching.
-   It is designed to help believers start the year strong and renew
    faith/ignite passion for God's purpose.
-   **2026 date caveat:** the dedicated GM2026 page is internally
    inconsistent: it is labelled Gaining Momentum 2026 but visibly shows
    "6th--11th January 2025" in its date heading. Do not quote a
    definitive 2026 date without human confirmation.
-   Source: https://www.davidschristiancentre.org/gm2026

### International Word of Faith Conference (IWOF)

-   Annual mid-year conference, normally in July.
-   DCC describes it as a one-week gathering with morning/evening
    sessions focused on faith teaching and practical faith knowledge.
-   **2026:** 7--12 July 2026; 5th edition.
-   Venues: Victory Dome, Amuwo Odofin (Mainland) and DCC Lekki/Island.
-   2026 speaker line-up reported in the knowledge base: Pastor Poju
    Oyemade, Rev. Tokunbo Adejuwon, Rev. Peter Alabi, Pastor Innocent
    Tamunokubie, Pastor Mildred Kingsley-Okonkwo, and Dr. Kingsley
    Okonkwo.
-   The 2026 edition was described as a prelude to DCC's
    30th-anniversary celebrations.
-   Source used for 2026 detail: THISDAYLIVE coverage dated 1 July 2026.
