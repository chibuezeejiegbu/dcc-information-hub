## Church identity

### How DCC describes itself

DCC describes David's Christian Centre as the **home of Victorious
people** and a church committed to raising victorious people by
schooling and training them through the Word of Faith into a life of
"100% victory 100% of the time."

The About page explains the name through the statement that, as David
never lost a battle, so will His walk with us be, citing 1 Chronicles
18:6b.

### Vision / purpose language

-   Raise a victorious people.
-   School and train people through the Word of Faith.
-   Emphasise applying Bible-based principles in practical Christian
    living.
-   DCC repeatedly frames itself around faith, victory, training, and
    practical Christian living.

### History

-   The supplied knowledge base states that DCC was **founded in 1996**,
    based on national press coverage of its 2026 30th-anniversary
    season.
-   **30th anniversary:** 2026, with celebrations culminating in
    September 2026.

## The 4Rs

### Results

DCC says victory is possible in every area of life and emphasises
experiencing results spiritually, financially, academically, in health,
marriage, and career.

### Real Life

DCC says Christian life can be enjoyable and describes its members as
fun-loving, down-to-earth, and real.

### Reach Out

DCC describes itself as a family that loves God and people, participates
in outreach, and uses Connect groups to foster fellowship.

### Right Living

DCC says the Bible is its manual and members are expected to live by the
Word daily.

## Outreach identity

DCC's Outreach page describes community outreach as a priority,
including youth empowerment, work with children, care for elders, free
healthcare services, school rehabilitation, environmental schemes, and
educational plans. It identifies **Christmas 1000X Better** as an annual
Christmas-season outreach and records historical projects including
provision of ICT infrastructure for Amuwo Community Secondary School in
2012.
